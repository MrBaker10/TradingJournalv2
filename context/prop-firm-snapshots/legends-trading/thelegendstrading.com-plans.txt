Our Legends Trading quick-start packages are easy to use. Select the Apprentice Evaluation trading packages that best meets your goals, skills, and comfort level. If you achieve the designated profit goal for the Apprentice account package you’ve selected, properly managing your risk and position size, and trade for only 1 day you are eligible to qualify for our Master Account.
What Happens if I Go Over My Daily Max Loss or EOD Trailing Max Loss Parameters?
The Legends Trading programs are designed to simultaneously test your trading strategy and risk management skills. If you exceed your EOD Max Drawdown on an Apprentice account, your current evaluation will be disqualified from funding. You will need to purchase a Reset or wait until the renewal of your subscription for a complimentary Reset for disqualified clients. On a Master or Straight To Master account, if you exceed your EOD Max Drawdown, your account will liquidated and you will need to purchase a Reset if you plan to continue trading.
