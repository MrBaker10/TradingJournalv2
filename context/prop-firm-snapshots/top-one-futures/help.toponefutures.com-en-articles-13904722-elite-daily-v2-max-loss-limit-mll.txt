Elite Daily V2 – Max Loss Limit (MLL)
This article explains about the Max Loss Limit (MLL) on the Elite Daily V2 Accounts
Table of contents
The Max Loss Limit (MLL) is the maximum total loss your account can sustain before it is permanently breached . Unlike the Daily Loss Limit, the MLL is based on an End of Day (EOD) trailing drawdown model.
Good to know: the MLL level moves up only at the end of the trading day, based on your closing balance, and locks in at your starting balance + $100 once your account has grown enough. The breach level is checked in real time. If your equity goes at or below it at any moment, the account is breached. There are no warnings when the MLL is breached.

How the End of Day Trailing Drawdown Works
The MLL follows a trailing model, but it only adjusts at the end of each trading day. This means:
Your breach level is active from the moment you start: starting balance minus your max drawdown (e.g. $50,000 − $2,000 = $48,000).
It trails upward as your account reaches new highs.
It moves up only at the end of the day, based on your closing balance. Intraday highs don’t move it.
It does not move down if your balance decreases.
The breach level is checked in real time. If your equity goes at or below it at any moment, the account is breached.
As your account grows, the drawdown level locks in at your starting balance + $100 and does not continue to trail.

What Happens If You Hit the Max Loss Limit?
If your account equity goes at or below the breach level at any time, the account is permanently breached and trading access is removed. There are no warnings when the MLL is breached.
