No Daily Loss Limit Add-On: Trade Without Daily Limits
Information about the No Daily Loss Limit Add-On
Table of contents
Have you ever been stopped out by a strict daily loss limit, only to watch the market reverse and head straight for your profit target right after?
Starting October 16, 2026 , traders can select the new No Daily Loss Limit add-on at checkout. This feature gives your strategy the breathing room it needs to execute without the pressure of a daily limit.
Supported account types
The No Daily Loss Limit add-on is currently available for:
Elite ACCESS
Elite Daily
Ignite Instant Funding
How pricing works
The add-on price depends on the account type you choose:
Account type
Price with the add-on
Elite ACCESS
Evaluation price: same price as an account with a daily loss limit.
Activation fee: increases by approximately 30%. You only pay the extra fee after you have passed.
Elite Daily
Checkout price: the base account price increases by approximately 30% at checkout when you turn on the add-on.
Ignite Instant Funding
Checkout price: the base account price increases by approximately 30% at checkout when you turn on the add-on.
Note: full account pricing and activation fee details can be found on our homepage and checkout page.
How to add it
Select "None" under the Daily Loss Limit toggle during checkout to trade with no daily loss limit.
Important: the No Daily Loss Limit add-on can only be selected during the initial checkout of your account purchase. It cannot be applied afterwards, during the activation fee payment, or when paying for an account reset.
