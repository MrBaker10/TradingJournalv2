Maximum Payout Until Live Explained
The maximum amount you can withdraw per payout in the Sim Funded stage, per account type and size, until you move to a Live Account.

Until traders transition to a Live Account , there is a Maximum Payout Limit in place. This article explains how this works and what traders can expect.
What is the Maximum Payout Until Live?
While in the Sim Funded stage, traders can withdraw profits, but there is a limit on how much can be withdrawn in a single payout. Once a trader qualifies for a Live Account, this limit is removed, and traders can withdraw without restriction.

If your profit exceeds the maximum for your account, you can simply request the maximum amount, and the remaining profit stays in your account to be withdrawn in a future payout request.
Does the maximum payout apply to every withdrawal?
Yes. While in the Sim Funded stage, every withdrawal is subject to the payout limit based on your account size. However, once you transition to a Live Account, there are no payout restrictions.
Where can I track my progress toward a Live Account?
You can monitor your performance and payout eligibility in the Top One Futures trader dashboard, which provides real-time updates on your trading status.
