Elite Daily V2 – Profit Target (Evaluation)
This article explains about the Profit Target Requirement during the Elite Daily V2 Evaluation Phase
Table of contents
The Profit Target defines the amount of net profit required to successfully pass the Elite Daily evaluation. Elite Daily is a one phase evaluation model, meaning you only need to reach one profit target to qualify for funding.
Important: although there is no minimum day requirement, the 40% Consistency Rule must still be respected. No single trading day may account for more than 40% of your total profit at the time of passing.

How the Profit Target Works
The target is based on the starting balance
The required profit is 6%
Profit must be based on closed PnL
Floating profits do not count
All trades must be closed for the account to be reviewed
40% Consistency Rule Still Applies
Although there is no minimum day requirement, the 40% Consistency Rule must still be respected: no single trading day may account for more than 40% of your total profit at the time of passing. See Elite Daily V2 – 40% Consistency Target (Evaluation Only) for the full details.
