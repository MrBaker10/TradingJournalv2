Can I Hold Trades Overnight?
The no overnight holding rule: all trades must be closed by 4:10 PM EST, and what happens if positions are left open.
Table of contents
At Top One Futures, we prioritize risk management. As part of our trading rules, holding trades overnight is not allowed . All trades must be closed by 4:10 PM EST daily.
Can I hold trades overnight?
No , traders must close all positions by 4:10 PM EST. Any open trades left running past this time may result in violations and potential account penalties.
