Elite Daily V2 – Daily Payout Rules
Accounts purchased on or after 5/11/26
Table of contents
One of the core advantages of the Elite Daily Plan is the ability to request payouts daily once funded. There is no consistency target on Elite Daily funded accounts. Funded traders may request a payout every 24 hours, provided all payout conditions are met.
In short: daily payouts once funded, 90/10 profit split, $500 minimum, a per-request maximum and a fixed buffer per account size, and after your first payout at least 50% of every payout must come from new profits (Daily Progression Rule).
Payout Rules at a Glance
Rule
Details
Payout eligibility
Evaluation: no. Funded: yes, daily (every 24 hours, provided all payout conditions are met).
Profit split
90% Trader / 10% Firm on all funded Elite Daily accounts.
Minimum payout
$500. Requests below $500 will not be processed.
Maximum payout per request
See table below. Profits above the maximum payout cap remain in the account.
Payout buffer
A fixed buffer must remain in the account at all times. Only profits above the buffer are eligible for withdrawal.
Daily Progression Rule (50% rule)
After your first payout, at least 50% of every future payout request must come from new profits generated since your previous payout. This requirement resets after every approved payout.

$25K account ($1,500 buffer, $750 max payout)
Your balance reaches $28,000 and you request $750. New balance: $27,250. Since the account remains above the $26,500 required level, the payout is eligible.
To request another $750, at least $375 must come from new profits. Your balance must grow from $27,250 to $27,625 before another full payout request becomes eligible.

$100K account ($3,500 buffer, $1,500 max payout)

Path to Live (5 Payouts)
The goal during the Sim Funded stage is consistent performance. After successfully completing 5 payouts , traders will typically become eligible to transition to a Live account. While traders may occasionally be transitioned sooner, 5 successful payouts remains the standard guideline.
Important Clarifications
The 50% rule applies to every payout request after the first payout
The rule resets after each approved payout
Buffer requirements always remain active
The payout cap applies per request
How Payout Review Works
Before approval, the following is reviewed: Max Loss Limit compliance, 50% Progression Rule compliance and News Rule compliance. All requirements must be met simultaneously.
What Happens After a Payout?
Account balance adjusts accordingly
End of Day trailing drawdown remains active
Buffer requirements remain active
Next payout eligibility begins the following trading day
Important Notes
Daily payouts do not override risk rules
Payout caps remain enforced
Only closed profits count toward payouts
Rule violations may result in payout denial
