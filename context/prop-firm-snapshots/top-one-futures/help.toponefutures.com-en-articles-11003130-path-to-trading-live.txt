Transitioning to Pre-Live / Live
After 3 successful payouts from one sim funded account, a risk manager will review your account and either keep you in the sim funded environment for further review, or transition you to a live account. Top One Futures may transition you to a live account sooner than your third payout depending on your trading history.
When transitioning to a live / pre-live account, all other accounts are temporarily disabled while the risk manager evaluates the trader's performance. Trading on sim funded accounts is not permitted during this review period.

What is the Pre-Live stage and why does it exist?
Based on trader behavior and breach patterns, many successful SIM traders struggle with the psychological transition to live trading. To bridge that gap, we created the Pre-Live stage : an opportunity for you to continue earning sim profits with the same benefits of the live program, while making the shift to trading fully live capital.
The Pre-Live environment allows us to:
Assess how a trader handles live-market psychology and risk management under real-time pressure with the same starting balance as their sim account.
Protect firm capital from immediate exposure to high-risk behavior.
Smooth out the Live transition by evaluating traders in a realistic yet controlled setting.

Frequently asked questions
How many payouts do I need before I can go live?
For most accounts, you need 3 successful payouts. Elite Daily accounts are the exception and require 5 successful payouts. In some cases a trader may be transitioned to a Live Account sooner, but the numbers above are the standard requirement.
If I get selected to move to live, can I choose to stay in Sim Funding?
No. If you are selected to move to live, your options are to begin trading live or to close your Sim Funded account. You may not voluntarily close your live account and move back to Sim. Sim trading should be seen as a gateway to trading live, having your consistency rule removed, receiving unlimited payouts, and scaling your capital. Any attempts to intentionally breach accounts to remain in Sim is strictly prohibited and may result in having trading privileges revoked.
What are the benefits of trading a Live account versus Sim?
If you are selected to move to trading live, you have the ability to earn an unlimited income with no withdrawal limits as a professional trader. Not only will your consistency rule be removed, you will have the ability to withdraw profits every 24 hours.
How long does it take to receive my live account?
Once you have been approved to move to live, your live account will be set up with the broker in 2 to 3 weeks.
What is the minimum withdrawal?
The minimum withdrawal amount is $200.
Can I withdraw from my starting balance?
No. Withdrawals are available from profits created on top of your starting balance. Example: on a live account, you will start with a $0 balance. You must have a positive balance in order to withdraw.
Can I purchase or continue to trade an Evaluation account or Sim Funded account if I am trading in a live account?
No, once you are trading live, you may not purchase additional accounts unless you are in the Pre-Live stage. If you are in the Pre-Live stage, you may purchase and trade on one Evaluation account. However, you will not be eligible for payouts on that Evaluation account, and you will not be able to upgrade to a SIM Funded account while in Pre-Live.
What will happen to my Sim Funded accounts when I am in a live account?
If you have an active account when you transition to a Live Account, that account will be closed.
What if I lose my live account?
Should you lose your Live Account, the risk management team will determine the appropriate next steps for the trader, based on their trading activities across all accounts associated with their profile.
What are the costs in the Live account?
There are no account or platform costs in live, but as a live trader, you are responsible for the professional market data. You will need to subscribe to receive Professional Data Subscription(s). Top One Futures does not make a profit on these data fees. Our trading platform charges us these fees that are passed along to you as the trader.
