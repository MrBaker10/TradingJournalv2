Elite DAILY V2 - Overview
This article gives an overview of Elite Daily V2 accounts launched on 5/11/26
Table of contents
The Elite Daily V2 Plan is a one phase evaluation model built for disciplined futures traders who want an End of Day trailing drawdown, daily payout eligibility, structured contract scaling and fast progression to funded status. Available account sizes: $25,000, $50,000, $100,000 and $150,000 .
Key highlights: one phase evaluation, End of Day trailing drawdown, 40% consistency rule during the evaluation only, daily payouts from Day 1 with a 50% Daily Progression Rule, 90/10 profit split, no activation fee and a maximum of 5 funded accounts per trader.

Key Rule Highlights
Rule
Details
One Phase Evaluation
Only one phase is required to pass.
End of Day Trailing Drawdown
The breach level trails at the end of the trading day with your highest closed balance, not intraday. The breach level is checked in real time. If your equity goes at or below it at any moment, the account is breached. See Max Loss Limit (MLL) .
Daily Loss Limit
A Daily Loss Limit applies during both the evaluation and funded phases: $500 on $25K, $1,000 on $50K, $1,250 on $100K and $1,850 on $150K.
Daily Payouts
Funded traders may request payouts daily, starting from Day 1.
Consistency Rule (40%, Evaluation Only)
No single trading day may account for more than 40% of total profits during the evaluation phase.
Daily Progression Rule
For each payout request, at least 50% of the payout amount must come from profits generated since your last payout. Example: if requesting $1,000, at least $500 must be new profit generated since the previous withdrawal.
News Rule (Funded Only)
No trades may be executed or closed 2 minutes before and 2 minutes after major high-impact news events.
No Activation Fee
Unlike other plans, Elite Daily has no activation fee.
Purchase Guidelines
To support disciplined trading and maintain fair participation across the platform, the following limits apply:
Traders may purchase up to 10 evaluation accounts within any rolling 30-day period starting from the initial purchase date
Each evaluation account may be reset a maximum of 12 times within a 30-day window
A trader may maintain no more than 5 active funded accounts at any given time
Important Notes
Maximum 5 funded accounts per trader
Consistency rule applies only during the evaluation phase
Resets are available for both evaluation and funded accounts
Contract scaling rules apply once funded
