Elite Daily V2 – Contract Scaling Plan
This article explains about the Contract Scaling Plan on the Elite Daily V2 Funded Phase
Table of contents
The Contract Scaling Plan applies only to funded Elite Daily accounts. There are no scaling restrictions during the evaluation phase. Once funded, your maximum contract size increases as your account equity grows. Your allowed contract size updates based on your closing balance at the end of each trading day .
Good to know: contract increases apply the following trading day after your closing balance qualifies. Intraday profits do not unlock higher scaling tiers until the day closes, and scaling never overrides the DLL or MLL rules.
Key Principles
Scaling applies only to funded accounts
No scaling requirements during evaluation
Contract limits update daily based on end of day balance
Scaling is based on simulated profits
Traders must remain within the allowed contract size at all times

How Scaling Is Calculated
Scaling tiers are determined by your funded account closing balance, your total simulated profit and your end of day equity. Contract increases apply the following trading day after your closing balance qualifies. Intraday profits do not unlock higher scaling tiers until the day closes.
Trader Responsibility
It is the trader's personal responsibility to monitor and follow the clearly outlined contract scaling rules on funded accounts. Exceeding your allowed contract limit is considered a rule violation. If the contract scaling rules are not followed:
Trades may be rejected or reduced
The account may be flagged for review
Profits generated while exceeding contract limits may be removed
Payout requests may be deemed ineligible
Compliance with scaling rules is mandatory for payout eligibility.
Important Clarifications
Scaling applies per individual account
Contract limits do not combine across multiple accounts
Simulated profit determines scaling tier
Contract limits are based on end of day balance
Scaling does not override DLL or MLL rules
