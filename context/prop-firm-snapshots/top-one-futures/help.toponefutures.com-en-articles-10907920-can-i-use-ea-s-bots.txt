Can I Use EA's/Bots?
Our EA and Bot policy: automated trading is not allowed on any account, and what happens if it is detected.
Table of contents
At Top One Futures, we do not allow the use of Expert Advisors (EAs) or Bots on our accounts. This article explains the policy and the consequences of breaking it.
Can I use EAs or Bots to trade on my account?
No , the use of Expert Advisors (EAs) or Bots is not allowed on your accounts. We require all traders to engage in manual, skill-based trading to ensure fairness and integrity in the trading environment.
What happens if I use EAs or Bots?
If you are found using an EA or Bot on your account, you may face the following consequences:
Consequence
Detail
Account suspension or termination
Your account may be suspended or closed
Loss of funding privileges

Trading status
Possible violations that may affect your trading status
By adhering to this policy, traders at Top One Futures can trade with confidence, knowing they are following the best practices for fair and risk-managed trading.
