Elite Daily V2 – Daily Loss Limit (DLL) - Funded Phase
In this article you can find more about the Daily Loss Limit during the Funded Phase of the Elite Daily V2 accounts
Table of contents
The Daily Loss Limit (DLL) is set between 1.23% and 2.0% of your account balance, depending on the account size, for both the evaluation phase and the funded phase of the Elite Daily V2 plan. This helps manage risk and ensures responsible trading. Here's how it looks for each Elite Daily funded account.
💡 Prefer to trade without a Daily Loss Limit? You can select the No Daily Loss Limit add-on at checkout for about 30% more than the base account price. The add-on can only be chosen during the initial checkout, not afterwards or when paying for a reset. Learn more: No Daily Loss Limit Add-On .
Important disclaimer: the Daily Loss Limit is a risk management tool, not a stop loss . It may execute late or early, fill at a different price than expected, or not trigger exactly at the set value. Place hard stop loss orders directly on your trades; any loss beyond your allowed threshold remains the trader's responsibility.

How Is It Calculated?
The Daily Loss Limit is a dynamic value calculated based on your previous day's closing balance. Let's use an example to illustrate, with a $25K account where the daily loss allowed is $500:
Step
Amount
Previous day's close
$26,000
Daily loss allowed
$500
Breach level for the current day
$26,000 - $500 = $25,500
This means that if your account's equity drops to $25,500 at any point during the current trading day, the system will automatically execute market orders to liquidate all open positions.
Important Disclaimer
The Daily Loss Limit (DLL) is a risk management tool, not a stop loss. It should never be relied on to close your trades at a specific loss amount. Due to market volatility, slippage, and execution speed:
The DLL may execute late
It may execute early
It may fill at a different price than expected
It may not trigger exactly at the set value
If you want to control your risk properly, you must place hard stop loss orders directly on your trades. Relying solely on the DLL can result in larger losses than intended. Top One Futures is not responsible for any losses that exceed the daily drawdown limit. Any loss beyond your allowed threshold remains the trader's responsibility.
