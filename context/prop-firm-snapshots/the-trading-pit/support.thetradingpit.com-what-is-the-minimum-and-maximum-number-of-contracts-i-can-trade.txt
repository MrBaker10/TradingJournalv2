Which Platforms Can I Use For Futures Prime?
Futures Prime is supported on ATAS, Quantower, Volsys and R/Trader, plus Tradovate, NinjaTrader and TradingView.
Some platforms are compatible but unsupported and need your own licence (Sierra Charts, MotiveWave, Jigsaw and Volfix). Mobile trading is available.
Can I use Replikanto with NinjaTrader?
Yes, but only Replikanto FlowBots – Compliance Edition is permitted. Standard, modified, or any other non-compliance versions of Replikanto are not allowed.
How Do I Set Up My Platform?
All Futures Prime trading is on a demo account.
To connect, log in to your chosen platform with the demo credentials The Trading Pit sends you — your login/username, password and the server (system) to connect to.
Use the connection guide for your platform, and the vendor’s own help for using the platform itself:

What About Market Data?
With futures, pricing comes directly from the exchange in real time. Level 1 (top of book) is included free on all accounts; deeper market depth (Level 2) is a paid monthly subscription:
Level 1 (Top of Book) — the highest bid and lowest ask, either across multiple exchanges (NBBO) or on a single exchange.
Level 2 (Depth of Book) — multiple price levels of bids and asks. Available as Market-by-Price (aggregated volume at each price) or Market-by-Order (every individual order at each level).
Which Provider Your Account Uses
You choose your trading platform at purchase — the market data provider is set automatically from that platform, not chosen separately.
By default accounts use DxFeed, except R/Trader Pro (which uses Rithmic) and Tradovate/NinjaTrader (which use Tradovate).
Once your account is created, the platform and data provider can’t be changed.

Professional vs Non-Professional
When you subscribe in your dashboard, select Non-Professional status.
Professional status is charged significantly higher fees.
This applies to DxFeed, Rithmic and Tradovate data.
What Are The Commissions?
Standard and mini contracts: $0.50 per contract.
Micro contracts: $0.20 per contract, except Micro Bitcoin at $0.50.
Which Instruments Can I Trade?
Futures Prime covers a wide range of instruments across several exchanges, including
Currency futures (e.g. 6E, 6B, 6J).
Energy (CL, NG, RB).
Equity indices (ES, NQ, YM, RTY and their micros).
Metals (GC, SI, HG).
Agriculture (ZC, ZS, LE), interest rates.
Micro crypto (MBT, MET).
European instruments (DAX/FDAX, EURO STOXX 50/FESX, Euro-Bund/FGBL).
How Many Contracts Can I Trade?
There is no minimum.
The maximum depends on your account size
$50K up to 5 standard / 50 micro
$100K up to 10 / 100
$150K up to 15 / 150
The lower your current balance, the fewer contracts you can trade.
