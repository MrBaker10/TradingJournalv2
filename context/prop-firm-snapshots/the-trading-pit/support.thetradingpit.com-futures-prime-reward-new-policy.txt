What Is The Futures Prime Reward Policy?
On Futures Prime you can receive a reward of up to 80% of the notional profit you generate in the Earning Phase.
Eligibility
First reward: complete 5 profitable trading days, each with at least $150 profit, before your first reward request.
Second and later rewards: generate positive net profit of at least $0.01 since your last payout, and complete 5 new trading days each with at least $150 profit.
Note: the daily profit threshold was lowered from $200 to $150 per day on 27 July 2026; all other conditions are unchanged.

How Are Rewards Paid?
Withdrawal methods, currencies and reward fees follow The Trading Pit’s general reward policy — rewards are paid by bank wire or cryptocurrency. See our general Rewards information for currencies and any transaction fees.
