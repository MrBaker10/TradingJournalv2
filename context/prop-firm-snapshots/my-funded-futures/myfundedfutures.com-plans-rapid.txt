STRAIGHT TALK
Yes, Rapid uses intraday trailing drawdown.
Here's what that actually means — and why we'd rather explain it upfront instead of hiding it in the fine print.
The Drawdown Doesn't Trail Forever
Intraday trailing drawdown only applies while building your safety buffer.
After building your buffer above the starting balance +$100, the trailing threshold stops moving and becomes static — it stops trailing upward permanently.
That means after the buffer is secured:
Your drawdown no longer trails intraday
Risk parameters become more stable
You can trade without the floor continuing to climb
We could hide that in the fine print. Most firms do. Instead, we'd rather you understand the trade-off before you start.

FAQ
Questions traders actually ask.
How does the 90/10 profit split work on the Rapid plan?
You keep 90% of every payout. MFFU keeps 10%. There's no graduated tier, no 'first $X at one rate, then a different rate.' Every payout you request on a Rapid account follows the same 90/10 split.
How fast can I get my first payout on Rapid?
The evaluation requires two trading days. Once funded, you need to build the required buffer ($2,100 on the $50K account) in realized profits before your first payout request. After that threshold is cleared, you can request every 24 hours. For most traders, first payout eligibility comes a few days into the funded stage — it depends entirely on how quickly you build the buffer.
What is intraday trailing drawdown?
Intraday trailing drawdown means your max-loss floor moves up with your equity in real time during the session — not just at end-of-day. On Rapid Sim Funded, once your balance is +$100 above your starting balance after your first payout, the floor locks and stops trailing upward permanently.
Does the Rapid plan have a daily loss limit?
No. Rapid has no daily loss limit in either the evaluation or the funded stage. The only loss constraint is your max drawdown — that's it.
What's the difference between Rapid and the other MFFU plans?
Rapid is built for speed: daily payouts, 2-day pass, 90/10 split, $0 activation fee. Builder is the low-cost entry plan with a structured 5-payout ladder to a live brokerage seat. Pro has the largest account sizes and highest per-payout cap. Flex is a separate plan available in $25K and $50K sizes — it has different payout mechanics. Rapid is the plan MFFU recommends for traders who want the fastest payout cadence and best profit split.
Is there an activation fee on Rapid?
No. Rapid has zero activation fees at every account size. When you pass the evaluation, your funded account activates instantly — you don't pay anything additional to unlock it.
Can I hold positions overnight on Rapid?
No. Rapid is intended for intraday futures trading. All positions must be closed before the daily session close. Holding overnight will breach the account.
What happens if I hit the max loss on Rapid?
On a Rapid Evaluation, breaching the max drawdown fails that evaluation, but you can reset it for a specific fee and start trading again. On a Rapid Sim Funded account, breaching the max drawdown closes the account. Funded accounts can't be reset, so you'll need to start a new evaluation. The max drawdown is a hard line, so we recommend trading well above it.
Why is Tier 1 news trading restricted on Rapid?
Tier 1 news events (CPI, NFP, FOMC, etc.) create extreme short-term volatility that can blow out simulated accounts before stop-losses fill. To keep the simulated market behavior representative of live execution risk, T1 events are restricted within 2 minutes before and after the release.
How many Rapid accounts can I have at the same time?
For $25K and $50K Rapid accounts, you can hold up to five active sim funded accounts at once. For $100K and $150K accounts, the limit is three total sim funded accounts across all plans.
