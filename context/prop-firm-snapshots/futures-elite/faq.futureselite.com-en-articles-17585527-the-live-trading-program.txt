How you qualify
Our Risk team decides who moves to a Live account. It usually happens after the 4th consecutive payout, but it can happen earlier depending on our risk review. Live payouts are daily, with a $200 minimum and a 90% split. Other paths are large withdrawals over time, outstanding Sim Funded results, or previous live trading. None of these is a guarantee: if you're selected, we contact you directly with full details.
What transfers with you
Your funded-account profit converts to live capital up to a cap for your account size. Anything above the cap doesn't carry over. Converted money goes into your Reserve , which you earn back by trading well.

Several funded accounts
All your funded accounts convert, but they go live one at a time. The first starts at $0 with its own floor and cushion; the others wait in a queue with their money in Reserve. Every 10 profitable trading days, the next account can go live after a risk check.
