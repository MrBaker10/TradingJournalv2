Reset fees
What a reset does and what it costs.
Table of contents
A reset clears your drawdown and returns the balance, trading days, consistency and every other parameter to the start. You can reset in the evaluation and after a breach, and resume trading immediately. Resets are available at the prices shown for each plan. Funded accounts can be reset up to 5 times. Elite, Ultra and Oracle accounts created before 23 July 2026 cannot be reset.
