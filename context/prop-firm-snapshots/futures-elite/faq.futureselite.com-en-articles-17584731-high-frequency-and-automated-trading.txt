High-frequency and automated trading
Why HFT is prohibited.
High-frequency trading means automated strategies that submit and execute large numbers of orders in seconds or milliseconds. It is strictly prohibited.
It strains the platform, slows execution and data for every trader, and doesn't show discretionary skill. Automated detection monitors every account.
Account termination, immediately and without warning.
HFT profits voided.
Passed evaluations reviewed.
Permanent restriction for repeated or severe cases.
If you think your account was flagged by mistake, send an appeal to Support.
