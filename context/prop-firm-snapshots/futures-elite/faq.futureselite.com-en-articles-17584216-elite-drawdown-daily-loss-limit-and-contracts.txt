Elite drawdown, daily loss limit and contracts
How the Max Loss Limit trails and locks, and how contract scaling works.
Table of contents
End of Day trailing drawdown
The Max Loss Limit is based on your closed balance at the end of each trading day.
A profitable day can move it up. A losing day leaves it where it is. Intraday swings and open profit never move it.
25K: once the account has made $1,000 of profit, the floor locks at $25,000 and stops trailing.
50K, 100K, 150K: the floor locks at $100 above the starting balance.
Close a day at or below the Max Loss Limit and the account is breached. A breach is final: reset the account or start a new challenge.
Daily loss limit
You choose at checkout, on every size: no daily loss limit (the default), or a soft-lock daily loss limit of $600 (25K), $1,200 (50K), $1,800 (100K) or $2,700 (150K). Choosing it lowers the price.
If you choose it, it applies in the evaluation and once funded. The level is measured from the higher of your balance and equity at the previous daily close.
A soft lock is not a breach. If your equity reaches it, trading is locked for 24 hours from that moment; the account stays open.
Daily loss limit: on Elite only if you chose it at checkout; always on for Prime, Ultra and Oracle; Nitro and Instant have none.
Contracts
In the evaluation you have the full allowance from day one: 2, 4, 8 or 12 contracts. Once funded, contracts scale with profit:
Size
Contracts by profit on the account
25K
1 below $1,000 · 2 from $1,000
50K
2 below $2,500 · 3 from $2,500 · 4 from $3,499
100K
2 below $2,000 · 4 from $2,000 · 6 from $3,499 · 8 from $4,499
150K
3 below $2,500 · 6 from $2,500 · 9 from $4,499 · 12 from $5,499
The limit counts contracts held at the same time across all instruments. Going over it is a rule breach.
