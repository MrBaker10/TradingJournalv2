News trading
Tier-1 news in evaluation and funded accounts.
Table of contents
Evaluation: Tier-1 news trading is allowed.
Funded: a trade placed in a restricted news window is voided, not a breach. The account stays active and that trade doesn't count.
Nitro Sim Funded: Tier-1 news trading is not permitted.
Tier-1 events
Tier-1 events include employment reports, CPI, EIA reports for energy traders and key agricultural reports for agricultural traders. FOMC Minutes are no longer Tier 1. Use the Economic Calendar in your dashboard: events it doesn't mark as Tier 1 are not restricted.
