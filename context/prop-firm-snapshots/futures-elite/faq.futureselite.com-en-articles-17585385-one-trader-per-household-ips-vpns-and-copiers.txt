One trader per household, IPs, VPNs and copiers
Rules that keep every account to one trader.
Only one trader per household. Different traders using the same IP address is prohibited.
If you use a VPN, VPS, trade copier, copy trading software or any tool that routes your connection through a shared or dynamic IP, tell Support in advance .
If you can't explain shared-IP activity, it is treated as an IP-sharing violation and the account may be closed or reset.
Logging in from several countries may be flagged as account sharing.
Copying trades between your own accounts with our in-house copy trader is allowed.
