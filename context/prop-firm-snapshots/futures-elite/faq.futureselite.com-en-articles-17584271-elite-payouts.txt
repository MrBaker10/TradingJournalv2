Elite payouts
How much you can request and when.
Table of contents
When you can request
At least 6 profitable trading days in the current payout cycle, each above the minimum for your size ($100, $150, $250 or $350).
At least $1 of net profit in the cycle: your balance must be above where the last payout left it, or above the starting balance for the first payout.
Completed KYC.
Calendar time isn't enforced separately: the 6 days set the gap between payouts.
How much
You can request 50% of all profit remaining on the account (current balance minus starting balance), up to your size's cap. The minimum request is $500, so you need at least $1,000 of profit.

Accounts bought before 25 June 2026, 15:00 CET use the previous rule: 50% of the profit made in the current payout cycle only.
After a payout the balance drops by the gross amount withdrawn. The profit split is 90%, applied automatically. Split upgrades aren't sold at checkout, but some Elite Pack cards and Keeper skills can raise a funded account's split.
