Payments, refunds and the one-time fee
How to pay and our refund policy.
Table of contents
All challenges are a one-time payment: no subscription and no activation fee.
Accepted payment methods: card, crypto, Revolut Pay and ACH bank transfer.
The card must be in your own name: paying with another person's card is not allowed.
ACH payments take 3–5 business days to settle and your accounts are created automatically when it settles; please don't cancel or dispute the transfer.
There is no time limit to pass an evaluation.
Refunds
Sales are final once login credentials are issued. Exception: an account that has never been traded, requested within 14 days of purchase, can be refunded to the original payment method minus a $35 fee per account, after review by our billing team.
If you still have an older subscription, it renews until you cancel it in the Billing section of your dashboard.
