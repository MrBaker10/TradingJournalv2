Inactivity rules
How long an account can go without a trade.
Account
Rule
Standard accounts
Closed after 29 days without trading
Instant accounts bought after 23 Nov 2025
Closed after 7 days without trading
Live accounts
Closed after 29 days without trading
Instant accounts are closed after 7 days without trading; all other accounts after 29 days without trading. There is no time limit to pass an evaluation.
Need a pause for health, personal or technical reasons? Contact Support before the deadline.
