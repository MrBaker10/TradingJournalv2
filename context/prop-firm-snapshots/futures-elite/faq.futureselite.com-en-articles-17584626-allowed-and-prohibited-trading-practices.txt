Allowed and prohibited trading practices
Fair play rules for every account.
Table of contents
Allowed
Day trading, swing trading, scalping within our limits, Martingale and grid trading.
Manual, hands-on trading.
Copying trades between your own accounts, with no restrictions.
DCA, as long as it isn't your primary strategy.
Stop losses are not mandatory on simulated accounts, but we strongly recommend them. They are mandatory on live accounts.
Not allowed
Fully automated trading with AI or bots, and high-frequency trading.
Hedging or reverse trading, in one account, across your accounts, with other people, or with another firm.
Copying other traders, and coordinated group trading.
Placing several limit orders at one price to manipulate fills.
Profiting from isolated fills in gapped or illiquid markets, or tight brackets that rely on zero slippage.
Account flipping and rolling: payout, breach, payout again, or buying many accounts and gambling until one hits.
Different traders on the same IP address, and logins from several countries that suggest account sharing.
