Maximum Number of Accounts
How many Lucid Trading evaluation and funded accounts one trader can have active at the same time, and how the limit applies across product lines.

Lucid Trading offers several account types, each designed to support a different phase of the trader’s journey. Below are the rules and limitations associated with each account type.
Evaluation Accounts
Account Limit : Up to 10 active evaluation accounts per household or family.
Shared Limit : Traders may hold a maximum of 10 accounts in total across evaluation accounts and funded accounts.
Example : If you have 5 evaluation accounts, you may only hold 5 funded accounts.
Reserve Limit: You may keep 5 evaluation accounts in reserve while you have 5 funded accounts.
You may trade evaluation accounts while they are in reserve.
Funded Accounts
Account Limit : Up to 5 active funded accounts per household or family.
Shared Limit : Different types of funded accounts combined may not exceed 5 accounts total.
Example : If you have 3 LucidDirect accounts, you may only maintain 2 LucidPro funded accounts.
Live Account
Account Limit : Up to 5 active LucidLive accounts per household or family.
