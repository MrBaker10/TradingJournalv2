LucidFlex Customization
Overview
LucidFlex gives traders the ability to customize their account at the time of purchase. Two settings can be adjusted to create the trading experience that best fits your risk preferences. The customization options are:
Daily Loss Limit: On or Off
Consistency: 40% or 50%
These settings create four unique LucidFlex account configurations .
Daily Loss Limit
The Daily Loss Limit (DLL) determines whether a daily loss cap is enforced on your account. The DLL is a soft breach, so if you hit it you will simply be locked out until the next trading session.
Daily Loss Limit On
A Daily Loss Limit is enforced on both evaluation and funded accounts
Results in a lower evaluation purchase price
Your funded account gets On Demand Payouts (see below)
Daily Loss Limit Off
No Daily Loss Limit is applied during either the evaluation or funded phase
Results in a higher evaluation purchase price
Payouts follow the regular end-of-day schedule
Consistency
40% (standard): your largest day must be 40% or less of your evaluation profit. Lowest price.
50% (add-on): your largest day can be up to 50%, so you can pass in as few as 2 days. Added at checkout for a small fee.
Consistency applies to the evaluation only. Funded LucidFlex accounts have no consistency rule. See LucidFlex Consistency Percentage .
On Demand Payouts
Keep the Daily Loss Limit On and your funded account can request payouts during the trading session, from profit made that session (6:00 PM to 3:45 PM ET).
After requesting an intraday payout, trading on your funded LucidFlex account is paused until the end of the trading day.
Accounts with a Hedging Violation are not eligible for intraday payouts. See Prohibited: Hedging .
With the Daily Loss Limit Off , payouts follow the regular end-of-day schedule.
Available Account Configurations
LucidFlex offers four possible combinations.
Option 1: Daily Loss Limit ON + 40% Consistency
Daily Loss Limit enabled for evaluation and funded
On Demand Payouts
Cheapest version of LucidFlex
Option 2: Daily Loss Limit ON + 50% Consistency
Daily Loss Limit enabled for evaluation and funded
On Demand Payouts
Pass in as few as 2 days
Option 3: Daily Loss Limit OFF + 40% Consistency
No Daily Loss Limit
Regular end-of-day payouts
Option 4: Daily Loss Limit OFF + 50% Consistency
No Daily Loss Limit
Regular end-of-day payouts
Pass in as few as 2 days
Highest pricing version of LucidFlex
Choosing the Right Configuration
Your customization should reflect your priorities:
Choose Daily Loss Limit ON if you prefer a lower purchase price and On Demand Payouts
Choose Daily Loss Limit OFF if you want more flexibility and don't mind paying a premium for it
Choose 50% Consistency if you want to pass faster
Once selected, these settings cannot be changed for an active account. If you want a different configuration, you'll need to purchase a new LucidFlex account with your preferred options.
