Being Evaluated for Live
Lucid's risk team continuously monitors trader performance to determine when a trader is ready to transition to live futures trading.
Traders enter the live review pool when one or more of the following conditions are met:
After receiving the final payout (Payout 5) on their Flex, Pro, or Direct plan
After being paid out a significant amount of capital lifetime
If they earn the Maximum Daily Profit for their LucidDaily account size
If they have exceptional performance in sim funded
If they have previously been moved live
Payout 5 represents the maximum payout level, not a guaranteed minimum for live eligibility. This maximum payout level applies to Flex, Pro, and Direct plans.
All live transitions occur at the discretion of the Lucid risk team.
Cashout
A cashout option has been introduced for traders who do not yet feel comfortable trading live capital. This cashout option is equal to 50% of a traders live starting balance and is subject to a 90/10 profit split. If no choice to cashout is made within 24 hours of receiving the move to live email, your accounts will automatically move to Live.

Consistency Rules at Live
There are no consistency requirements for live accounts. Once promoted from sim-funded to live, consistency rules no longer apply regardless of your account type (LucidPro, LucidFlex, LucidDirect, LucidDaily).
Locking the Live MLL
Lucid Live accounts use an End-of-Day Drawdown (EOD Drawdown) system to calculate the Max Loss Limit (MLL).
At the end of each trading session, the system calculates the account’s highest closing balance.
The MLL increases as the account balance grows, up to a defined point.
Once a trader generates live profits equal to their starting live drawdown, the Max Loss Limit locks at $100 and no longer moves.
If a trader requests a live payout before generating profits over the live drawdown, the Max Loss Limit locks to $100.
Repeatable Live Bonus
A trader may be eligible to earn a repeatable bonus, based on their Live performance. The Live Bonus is subject to a 90/10 profit split.
To earn the Live Bonus, a trader must meet 2 objectives:
Generate Live Profits above the starting balance, equal to the Profit target outlined below.
Complete 20 trading days
A trading day is defined as 1+ trade taken, generating +$1/-$1 P&L net of fees at the end of the trading session. We strongly encourage legitimate trading activity, and exploitation of this bonus system, via "contract flipping" or similar, may lead to further review.
The Live Bonus Profit Target calculation is not negatively impacted by taking payouts.

Example
A trader is moved live with five $50,000 funded accounts:
Each account starts with $0 balance and $2,000 EOD drawdown

Cooldown
Traders who have been moved live may return to purchase a new evaluation after blowing a live account.
Standard cooldown period: 2 weeks
Traders who repeatedly blow live accounts due to reckless or "yolo" behavior may be subject to longer cooldowns at the discretion of the risk team

LucidLive Account FAQ
Do all funded prop accounts move live at once?
Yes. All eligible funded prop accounts (with at least one payout) are moved when a trader transitions to live.
What happens to remaining simulated prop accounts?
All simulated prop accounts are closed when a trader is moved live, or opts out of trading live via cash-out.
If a funded account is not moved live due to being ineligible, the evaluation cost of that account will be refunded, as long as the account has equal to or greater than 50% of the total drawdown remaining.
If you are not comfortable with evaluations being closed, do not keep evaluations in reserve.
If I am live, can someone in my household still trade sim?
No. If one household member is trading live, others may not trade simulated accounts.
Can I hedge my live accounts?
Absolutely not. Hedging live accounts is strictly prohibited by both Lucid and the CME. This includes any copytrader issues that may arise. Violations will result in a permanent ban.
