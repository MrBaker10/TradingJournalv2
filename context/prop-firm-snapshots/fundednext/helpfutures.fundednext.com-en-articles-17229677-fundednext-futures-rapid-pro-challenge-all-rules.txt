There is no Consistency Rule in the Challenge Account and no daily loss limit by default — though you can add one via the optional Daily Loss Limit Add-On at purchase. What is the FundedNext Futures Rapid Pro Challenge?
The Rapid Pro Challenge is one of two paths in the Rapid Pro & Daily model, built for flexibility and control . It has lighter restrictions during the Challenge Phase , letting you reach your target in a single day or across multiple days. Once you pass, you progress to a Rapid Pro FundedNext Account where you can withdraw Performance Rewards every 3 days.

Is there a Consistency Rule in the Rapid Pro Challenge?
No. The Consistency Rule does not apply during the Rapid Pro Challenge Account. You can meet the profit target in a single day or spread it across multiple days , depending on your trading style.
Note: A 40% Consistency Rule does apply once you reach your Rapid Pro FundedNext Account.

What is the Maximum Loss Limit in the Rapid Pro Challenge?
The Maximum Loss Limit (MLL) is the total amount you can lose before your account is permanently breached . It follows a Trailing End-of-Day (EOD) system, updating once per day based on your highest balance that day.

Important notes:
The MLL adjusts at the end of each trading day , not in real time.
It stops trailing and locks once your balance reaches the lock point for your size ($25,100 / $50,100 / $100,100).
If your floating loss breaches the MLL during a live trade, your account is breached instantly .

What is the Micro-Scalping policy for the Rapid Pro Challenge Account?
Micro-scalping is opening and closing trades within 10 seconds to capture small, rapid price movements. Scalping is valid, but a high concentration of these trades affects trading consistency, so FundedNext Futures monitors a Micro-Scalping threshold across all account types.
During the Challenge Phase :
The Micro-Scalping percentage is the share of your total recorded profit from trades closed within 10 seconds, evaluated per cycle .
Only profitable trades count — you can close losing trades any time without affecting this rule.
At 30% , you receive a compliance warning (no penalty).
At 40% or above , your ability to reach the profit target and advance is placed on hold ; it resumes automatically once your percentage drops back below 40%.

Is there a time limit to pass the Rapid Pro Challenge?
No. There is no time limit — take as long as you need to reach your profit target while following the rules.
What happens after I pass the Rapid Pro Challenge?
Once you meet the profit target while following the Maximum Loss Limit and all trading rules, you move to a Rapid Pro FundedNext Account , where the 40% Consistency Rule applies and you can withdraw Performance Rewards every 3 days.
