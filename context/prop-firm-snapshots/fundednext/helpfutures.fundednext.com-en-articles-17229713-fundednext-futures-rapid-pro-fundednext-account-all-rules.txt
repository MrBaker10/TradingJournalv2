TL;DR: On the Rapid Pro FundedNext Account a 40% Consistency Rule applies — your highest single-day profit must be 40% or less of total profit before a withdrawal is eligible (takes at least 3 days ). You also need $500 profit in the cycle. The Maximum Loss Limit still applies — 1,000(25K) , 2,000(50K) , 2,500(100K) . Rewards can be withdrawn every 3 days at a 90% Reward Share , and the account concludes after 5 Performance Rewards . You can earn Rising Trader, Champion Trader, and Limitless Trader certificates.
A Performance Reward is your share of the profits you earn for your skill and effort while trading a FundedNext Account after passing the Rapid Pro Challenge. (At FundedNext Futures, what other firms call a "payout" is called a Performance Reward .) With Rapid Pro, rewards can be withdrawn every 3 days once eligible. Here are all the rules that apply once you're funded.
Is there a Consistency Rule on the Rapid Pro FundedNext Account?
Yes. Unlike the Challenge Phase, the Rapid Pro FundedNext Account applies a 40% Consistency Rule . If your highest profit in a single day is more than 40% of your total profit, you'll need to keep trading until your results become more consistent.
There's no fixed number of days — it depends on your performance — but it takes at least 3 days to satisfy the rule. Once your highest profit day is 40% or less of total profit, you become eligible to withdraw.
Formula: Highest Profit Contribution = (Highest Profit Day ÷ Total Profit) × 100%
40% or less → rule met (withdrawal eligible).
More than 40% → rule not met (keep trading).

Is a buffer required on the Rapid Pro FundedNext Account?
No. The Rapid Pro FundedNext Account does not use a buffer rule. Withdrawal eligibility is based on the 40% Consistency Rule instead. The buffer rule applies only to the Rapid Daily model.

How much can I withdraw, and how often, from the Rapid Pro FundedNext Account?
You can withdraw a minimum of $250 up to your size-based maximum ( $800 / $1,200 / $2,500 ), provided you've met the 40% Consistency Rule and have at least $500 profit in the current cycle. Rewards can be withdrawn every 3 days .
What is my Reward Share from the Rapid Pro FundedNext Account?
You receive a 90% Reward Share of the requested Performance Reward.

What is the Rapid Pro FundedNext Account withdrawal journey?
Trade until your highest single-day profit is 40% or less of your total profit (at least 3 days) and you have $500 profit in the cycle.
Request a withdrawal (minimum $250 , up to your size cap), receiving a 90% Reward Share . Rewards can be requested every 3 days .
After your 5th Performance Reward , the Rapid Pro FundedNext Account is concluded .

What is the Micro-Scalping policy for the Rapid Pro FundedNext Account?
Micro-scalping is opening and closing trades within 10 seconds to capture small, rapid price movements. FundedNext Futures monitors a Micro-Scalping threshold across all account types.
On your FundedNext Account :
The Micro-Scalping percentage is the share of your total recorded profit from trades closed within 10 seconds, evaluated per cycle .
Only profitable trades count — you can close losing trades any time without affecting this rule.
At 30% , you receive a compliance warning (no deduction).
At 40% or above , the Micro-Scalping portion of your profit for that cycle is deducted in full . Your account is never terminated for micro-scalping, and future micro-scalping profit is subject to the same ongoing deduction.
If that deduction equals or exceeds your net profit for the cycle, no Performance Reward is issued and withdrawal is disabled for that cycle. A full breakdown appears in your dashboard.

How do I request my Performance Reward from the Rapid Pro FundedNext Account?
Once you've met all the eligibility criteria, you can request your Performance Reward at the end of the trading day by transferring your profits to your wallet.
Please note: placing a trade on the same day removes the transfer-to-wallet option for that day. So if you intend to request your Performance Reward, do it on a day when you haven't opened a new trade — otherwise you'll need to wait until the next eligible day to transfer.
