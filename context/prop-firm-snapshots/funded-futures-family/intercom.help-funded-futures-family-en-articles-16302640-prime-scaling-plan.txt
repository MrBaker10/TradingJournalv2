Prime Funded Account Scaling Plan
The Scaling Plan determines how much of your maximum contract size is available based on the simulated profits in your Funded Account. As your account profits increase or decrease, your available contract limit will scale up or down accordingly.
The Scaling Plan does not apply during the Evaluation phase.
Scaling limits are updated at the end of each trading session and will not adjust in real time throughout the day. Please refer to the table below for the applicable contract limits.
