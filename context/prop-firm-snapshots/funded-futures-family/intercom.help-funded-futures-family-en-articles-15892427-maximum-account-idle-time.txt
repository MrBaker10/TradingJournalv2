Maximum Account Idle Time
August 24, 2026
To keep your account active, you must place at least one trade per week (Monday–Friday) for a minimum of 10 seconds. This requirement applies to each account individually, not to users overall. If no trades are placed within this period, the account may be marked inactive and breached/closed.
Please note: If an account remains idle for one whole trading week or longer, it will be automatically marked inactive and closed by the system, and cannot be restored.
If you’re unable to meet this requirement or are planning on taking a break, just let us know ahead of time. We’re here to help and can work with you to keep your account active!
