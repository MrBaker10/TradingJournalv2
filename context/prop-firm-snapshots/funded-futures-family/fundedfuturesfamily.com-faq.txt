What is a prop firm challenge (evaluation)?
A prop firm evaluation is simply a chance to prove you can trade consistently. You trade in a simulated account that follows live market prices. Reach the profit target without breaking the risk rules, and you earn a funded account. At Funded Futures Family, you can pass both our Prime and Premier+ evaluations in as little as one trading day.
How do prop firms make money?
Prop firms make money from evaluation fees, account resets, and a share of the profits earned by funded traders. The best outcome for everyone is a trader who succeeds, because long-term payouts are more valuable than failed evaluations.
