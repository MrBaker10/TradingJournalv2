Are there any inactivity fees, or minimum trading requirements?
Modified on Wed, 30 Sep at 6:48 AM
No, we do not charge inactivity fees or have minimum trading requirements.
However, Tradovate has its own account inactivity system. If a user does not log into their Tradovate account for more than one calendar month, Tradovate will automatically archive the account. When this happens, the account is removed from the trading platform and cannot be recovered by us or reactivated. At this time the account will be considered failed, and a user will need to purchase a reset or buy a new account if they wish to continue trading.
In addition, any TradeDay account that remains dormant or inactive for 30 days will be considered inactive and may be offboarded.
How long do I have to submit my documentation after graduating to Funded Live?
Once you graduate from a Funded Sim account and are offered a Funded Live account, you will have 30 days from the date of graduation to submit the required documentation.
If the required documentation is not submitted within the 30-day period, the Funded Live account offer will be withdrawn.
