The EOD Challenge is a one-phase evaluation with end of day trailing drawdown and no time limit. Positions must be closed before the market closes.
Key benefits
No time limit to pass the evaluation
No daily loss limit during the evaluation phase
End of day trailing max drawdown, which locks once it reaches your starting balance
80/20 profit split by default, with an optional 90/10 add-on
$0 activation fee, so your funded account is activated at no extra cost
Pricing and targets
Two resets are available per challenge. You can hold up to 5 funded accounts.
How to pass
Reach the profit target of 6% of your starting balance.
Stay within the trading rules and loss limits below.
There is no minimum number of trading days. Once you pass, your evaluation period is reviewed, and your funded account is activated within 2 business days.
Maximum drawdown (end of day trailing)
Your maximum drawdown is the level your balance or equity cannot fall below. It trails up with your profits at the end of each trading day, and stops trailing once it reaches your starting balance. That lock applies in both the evaluation and funded phases.
Breaching it is a hard breach, which closes the account immediately.

Consistency rule
The consistency rule limits how much of your total profit can come from a single trading day, so that profits are steady and sustainable.
Evaluation: no single day may account for more than 50% of your total profit.
Funded: no single day may account for more than 30% of your total profit during a payout period.
Example on an EOD 50K. You make $1,500 in one trading day on your funded account. Your total profit for that period needs to reach at least $5,000 before you can request a payout, which puts your best day at 30% of the total.
If you go over the threshold: your account is not terminated. You simply cannot request a payout until your highest profit day falls back to the threshold or below. Keep trading and building profit until it does, then you are eligible again.
Payouts
Eligibility: every 7 winning days. A winning day is a net profit of at least 0.2% of your starting balance, so $100 on a 50K.
Request window: payout requests are submitted through your dashboard between the 5th and 8th, or the 20th and 23rd, of each month.
Minimum request: $500, across all account sizes. There is no profit buffer to build up first, so any amount above $500 qualifies.
Maximum request: 50% of your profit, capped by account size and payout number. The cap rises as you build a track record.

The remaining 50% of your profit stays in the account and supports your equity into the next payout cycle.
Profit split: 80/20 by default. You can add the optional 90/10 Profit Share Add-On for an extra 20% of the challenge price.
News trading
Evaluation: no restrictions.
Funded: a 2 minute buffer applies around high impact news events. You cannot open, close, or trigger pending orders within 2 minutes before or after a scheduled release. Positions opened before the window began may be held through the event.
Overnight and weekend holding
Overnight holding is not permitted. Any position still open is closed automatically at 15:55 CT, five minutes before the session ends.
Weekend holding is not permitted.
Microscalping
Profit from trades closed within 2 minutes of opening is not eligible. It is deducted from your account balance and from any payout calculation. The trade itself is not a breach.
This applies to EOD accounts in both the evaluation and funded phases. It is separate from the 2 minute news buffer above.
Tick scalping , meaning opening and closing positions within seconds to capture small tick profits, is not permitted on any account. Profit generated this way may be reviewed and deducted.
Inactivity
Place at least one trade every 7 days. If no trades are placed for 7 consecutive days the account is breached and closed, and the fee is not refunded. There is no time limit on passing the evaluation, but the account does need to stay active. If you are going to be away, tell support in advance.
